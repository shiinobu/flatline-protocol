The story

Why this case exists

Five weeks ago my sibling went into surgery. Should have been routine. Mid-procedure, the hospital's systems locked up — ransomware, dropped clean and fast. Whoever did it knew exactly what they were doing. The hospital paid to get the systems back online. My sibling never came out of that operating room. The official investigation ran for ten days, then just stopped. Nobody explained why. I stopped waiting for an explanation and started tracing the money instead. I'm not police. I'm not press. I don't have a client, a badge, or a deadline. I have one name — BLACKLEDGER — and I'm not stopping until I have every name behind it.

— GHOSTWIRE
